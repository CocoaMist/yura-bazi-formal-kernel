# Data licensing and attribution

Classical works are attributed to their historical authors as identified in each
source. Their Wikisource transcriptions and editorial contributions are attributed
to **Wikisource contributors**. Per-file book and history URLs, archived export
notices and SHA-256 hashes are supplied in `sources.jsonl` and `public_sources`.
No endorsement by Wikisource or the historical authors is implied.

The archived EPUB-export notice offers Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
3.0 and GNU Free Documentation License. For associated extracted TXT files this
distribution chooses **CC BY-SA 3.0**, preserving the original dual-license notice
as metadata. Files from raw wiki exports or separately resolved Wikisource pages
are distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**, according to the site's text license.
The ancient underlying works may independently be in the public domain.

- CC BY-SA 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- CC BY-SA 3.0 legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode
- CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- CC BY-SA 4.0 legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode
- Wikisource: https://zh.wikisource.org/
- Wikimedia terms: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use

The retained TXT files are byte-for-byte copies of the local archived extracts.
The compilation selects a redistributable subset; JSONL fields are projected,
offsets recomputed, and a fresh SQLite/FTS index built. Source versions were not
silently corrected or completed. No exact upstream revision ID is available.

New compilation metadata, database arrangement and first-party research
projections are offered by CocoaMist under **CC BY-SA 4.0**, to the extent those
rights exist. Quotations retain their source's applicable license. First-party
structural notes retain the project's MIT license, supplied alongside them.
No extra restriction is imposed on uses permitted by the applicable licenses.

Purchased texts, private case data and materials without established
redistribution permission are outside this package. The repository's root MIT
license grants no rights to those excluded materials.
